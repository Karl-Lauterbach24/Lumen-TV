# Lumen TV 1.13.1

## What is new

- **A 4K picture on a television that cannot decode it says so.** The Changhong tested decodes HEVC up to 1920×1088 only
  (OMX.MS.HEVC.Decoder): a 4K disc is read and decrypted, but its picture cannot be shown. The message names the ways that work:
  the Full HD Blu-ray of the box, or Lumen on a computer (which scales the picture down).
- **The drive reads at its full speed**: MakeMKV holds it at 9000 KB/s (SET CD SPEED); the drive shim asks for the maximum
  (`MMSHIM_SPEED=keep` leaves MakeMKV's value).
- **A drive that does not answer when it is opened gets a USB reset** (as if the cable were pulled). On the drive tested this resets
  its USB bridge, but not a drive that hangs behind it – that one still needs its power switched off.
