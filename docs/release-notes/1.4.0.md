# Lumen TV 1.4.0

## What is new

- **Dolby Digital, DTS (with DTS-HD) and TrueHD sound.** Many televisions decode none of these; the app
  now carries Media3's FFmpeg decoder (FFmpeg 6.0, LGPL), so films from discs have sound.
- **Choosing the audio and subtitle track** while a film from a drive plays: *Up* or *Menu* opens the list
  (language, format, channels; subtitles can be switched off). The television's language is preferred.
- **Disc identification and titles.** A disc read through MakeMKV is named (title, season, disc number from
  the disc's own data), recognised as a film or as episodes, and listed accordingly: the film, or
  *Episode 1 … n* plus *Play all* where the disc has such a title, and the extras. The disc page uses
  matching icons.
- **Experimental: LibreDrive discs through MakeMKV.** Where the user supplies the MakeMKV Linux files
  (they are not part of this package), the app runs them inside the app (proot), hands them the USB
  drive and plays the stream. Registration key: the user's own, or the forum's beta key (fetched daily).
  Cache between MakeMKV and the player in memory or on storage, automatic or chosen by hand. A drive that
  stops answering is ejected and loaded again once. Tested on one 1 GB television; memory is the limit.
- The new LUMEN logo (launcher icon, banner, page).

## Notes

- Without the MakeMKV files the MakeMKV entries do not appear; everything else works as in 1.3.2.
- BD-J menus (Java) are not supported.
