# Lumen TV 1.6.0

## What is new

- **Choosing the actual audio and subtitle track of a disc** (MakeMKV), before playing: the disc page
  has a page each for *Audio* and *Subtitles* that lists every track the disc has – also several in one
  language (another format, an old and a new dub, a forced subtitle track) – with language, format,
  channels and what the disc says about it. The choice is kept per disc.
  While a disc plays, the track list (Up / Menu) shows the same names; changing the track *during* a disc
  from MakeMKV is not possible (the disc is read once), so it says where to choose.
- **The disc's picture as the disc tile**, like on a games console, and beside the disc's page:
  series from TVmaze (no key needed); films from The Movie Database where the user enters a free key
  (*Settings › Disc and MakeMKV › Film covers*). Only the name is sent.
- A disc is named by what it is (*Doctor Who (2005) · Season 1 · Disc 3*) on its tile and its page;
  a title shows its number of chapters.
- Fixes: the start of a film is kept until the player has listed its tracks (a track chosen at the start
  needed the first bytes again); alike track names are numbered.

## Not in this version

- Chapter pictures: a stream from MakeMKV cannot be jumped in, and pictures need decoding.
- 3D (frame-sequential, DLP-Link) output: needs a renderer of its own and a television with 120 Hz.
- BD-J (Java) menus.
