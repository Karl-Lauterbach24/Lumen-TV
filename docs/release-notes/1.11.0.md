# Lumen TV 1.11.0

## What is new

- **4K discs (AACS 2) through the key database and LibreDrive.** Where MakeMKV on the television says "the volume key is unknown"
  but the community key database (FindVUK's KEYDB.cfg, *Load the key database*) has the disc's unit key, the app lets MakeMKV switch
  the drive's LibreDrive mode on (one full MakeMKV run, about two minutes) and then decrypts and plays the disc itself. Seen once with
  *Alita: Battle Angel* (HEVC picture and Dolby Digital sound came through); not yet reliable – see below.
- **Episodes are named again on a slow network.** One TVmaze show that does not answer in time (a long one such as the Doctor Who of
  1963) is skipped instead of ending the look-up; the names are kept per disc, so TVmaze is asked once.
- **A Blu-ray's DTS-HD Master Audio (stream type 0x86) no longer stops the player**: Media3 took it for SCTE-35 splice data.
- The empty player that stayed on the screen when the disc's menu ended by itself is closed.

## Fixed

- **Discs with bus encryption hung the drive** once the key database was loaded: the app tried to read them itself, authenticated
  with the revoked certificates of the public database, was refused, and the drive stopped answering. Such discs now go to MakeMKV
  as before; the app authenticates only with a certificate of the user's own (`hostcerts.cfg`).
- MakeMKV is no longer stopped in the middle of a command (that left the drive hanging).

## Not yet good

- 4K: the drive has to be in LibreDrive mode before the app reads the disc; when it is not (after the drive was plugged in again
  and before MakeMKV ran), reading hangs the drive. The order is enforced now, but this path has been through few tests.
- A diagnostic left switched on (`shimlog`) slowed MakeMKV down and filled the log; it is meant for tests only.
