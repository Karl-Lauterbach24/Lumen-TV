# Lumen TV 1.5.0

## What is new

- **Episodes in the right order.** MakeMKV numbers a disc's titles in no particular order. The order is
  now taken from the disc itself: the "play all" title runs through the episodes' clips in order (else
  the playlists' file names). Names from TVmaze then match what plays.
- **A disc is identified as soon as its page opens** (a protected Blu-ray the app cannot decrypt
  itself), and remembered by its label: the next time it is known at once. Episodes follow one another:
  when one ends, the next starts. *Extras* are a page of their own; the key database and the
  diagnostics are under *More options*.
- **Settings in groups**: *Interface* (sounds, menu music, screen saver), *Playback* (audio and
  subtitle language for every film), *Disc and MakeMKV* (key, cache) and *Connection* (name, address).
  The disc page keeps only what belongs to the disc.
- **The menu music loops without a pause** (it was a gap at each restart).
- The home page keeps your choice when a disc arrives (the cards used to move under the cursor).

## Not in this version

- BD-J (Java) menus. A Java runtime fits only a television with much more memory than the ones
  tested; see the repository's notes.
