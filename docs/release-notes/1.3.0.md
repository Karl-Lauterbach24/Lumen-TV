# Lumen TV 1.3.0

## What is new

- **Blu-ray menus on Android TV.** *Play the disc with its menu* starts the disc as a player would: its
  first-play program runs, its menu is shown over the picture, the remote's arrow keys, OK, the colour keys,
  *Menu* (pop-up menu) and *Top menu* work, and the buttons do what the disc says. This is libbluray
  (the library behind VLC and mpv) built into the app; the disc's files are read by the app itself, from
  a USB drive or an image, and AACS discs are decrypted as in 1.2.0.
- **4K / HDR10 discs**: the streams (H.265, 10 bit, PQ/BT.2020) are recognised, played and made into MKV
  files without change. *Playing needs a television whose decoder can do 4K H.265.*
- When the television cannot decode a picture, the app now says so on the screen instead of staying black.
- Titles that are only the second view of a 3D disc no longer appear in the list of titles.

## Checked, and limits

Run in the Android TV emulator (Android 14, ARM64) with discs made for the purpose:
a Blu-ray with a three-button HDMV menu (authored for the test and read by libbluray; the menu is shown,
the arrow keys move the selection, OK runs the button's command, the menu loops its clip until then and the
film plays after it), an AACS-encrypted disc through the same path, a 4K HDR10 H.265 clip made into MKV
(identical pictures to the original).

**Not tried**: a pressed retail disc with a real menu, a real disc drive, a 4K disc from a real drive, a
real television.

**Not supported, and said so on the screen:**
- **BD-J menus** (Java programs, on most Disney and many 4K discs): the disc's titles are listed instead.
- **BD+** discs: the app has no BD+ engine; their picture may be damaged or the disc refused.
- **Ultra HD discs** need a drive that can read them (a "LibreDrive"-capable drive); their key must be in
  the key database. The app does not do the drive-side steps; not tried.
- **Dolby Vision**: the base layer (HDR10) is played, the enhancement layer is dropped.
- Skipping and seeking inside a film started through the disc's menu: use the disc's own chapter menu;
  the *Titles* list (without the menu) seeks as before.
