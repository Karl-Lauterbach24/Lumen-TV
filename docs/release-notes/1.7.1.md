# Lumen TV 1.7.1

## What is new

- **The picture the disc carries is the disc's picture.** A Blu-ray holds its own cover (BDMV/META/DL,
  *Jacket_….jpg*); it is copied once and shown on the disc tile and beside the disc's page – no network, no key,
  and the right one for the box (the Ninth Doctor's, not another era's). TVmaze and TMDB remain the
  fallback for discs without one.
- **Save the disc's menu files** (*Disc page › More options*): the Java programs (BDMV/JAR, BDJO),
  metadata and clip information to the app's folder, to read what a disc's menu says about its tracks
  without running it. A disc with a Java menu (BD-J) keeps its texts in those files; a disc with a
  menu of pictures (HDMV, like the one tested) keeps nothing readable outside the encrypted film file.
