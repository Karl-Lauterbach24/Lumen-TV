# Lumen TV 1.8.0

## What is new

- **The names of a disc's tracks, read from the disc's own menu – by itself.** A disc's menu says which dub is which
  ("Deutsch – DTS-HD 5.1 MA (Synchronfassung 2013)", "… 2021", "Japanisch …"); the playlist only says "German,
  German, Japanese". After a disc has been identified, the app plays its menu in the background (libbluray, decrypted
  through MakeMKV's library, inside the app), finds the page with the audio and subtitle headings, reads the
  buttons by text recognition and checks them against the playlist's stream table. The names then stand in the
  *Audio* and *Subtitles* lists of the disc page and in the track list of the player. Once per disc, a few minutes;
  a film that is started meanwhile comes first.
- Needs MakeMKV's files (as the rest of the MakeMKV features); without them nothing changes.

## Not in this version

- The disc's menu itself on the screen, with its background and sound (the pieces are there: the menu is
  decrypted and drawn already).
