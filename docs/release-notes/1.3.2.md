# Lumen TV 1.3.2

## What is new

- **Disc drives on USB now work on real televisions.** Fixed: a drive's identity contained slashes, so
  its disc could not be addressed ("the disc is gone"); the list no longer waits for the storage
  permission; any bulk-only mass-storage interface is accepted, and a UAS bridge is switched to the
  bulk-only mode first; the drive is opened in the background ("reading the disc …") and a disc put
  in or taken out of a connected drive is noticed; a drive that spins down is no longer taken for a
  changed disc.
- **What the television shows is visible.** *USB drive* lists USB devices that were not taken (with
  vendor:product and interface classes) and what the kernel reports; OK on a row reads it in full.
- **The menu music starts with the intro** (it used to wait for the first key press). The *Disc* card
  is first on the home page.
- **Clear reasons when a disc does not play**: decoder limits, an unreadable stream, a key that is
  missing from the key database, and the protection of the disc (*Protection of this disc (AACS)*:
  the disc's ID, the key database, and whether the disc asks for bus encryption and the drive does it).
  Long messages wrap instead of running off the screen.
- Fixes in the menu engine bridge (events delivered with the stream, sounds of a disc's menu), the
  USB transport (commands that move no data), and the rest of the disc handling.

## Checked, and limits

Run in the Android TV emulator with discs made for the purpose, and on a real television with a USB
Blu-ray drive: the drive and disc are recognised, listed, and read.

**Limit: discs that ask for bus encryption.** Many newer Blu-rays ask the drive to scramble the data
on the cable; a drive that does so only releases the data to a player that identifies itself with a
certificate it accepts. The key database that the app loads contains only a certificate that current
drives have revoked, so on such a disc with such a drive the app cannot read the stream and says so
(*Protection of this disc* shows the drive's answer). Discs without bus encryption, DVDs and images
play; for the others, play the files of a rip (MKV), or play the disc with Lumen on a computer and
cast it (*From Lumen*).

Other limits as before: no BD-J menus, no BD+, no seeking inside a film started through its menu,
Ultra HD discs need a drive that can read them.
