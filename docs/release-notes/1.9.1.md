# Lumen TV 1.9.1

## What is new

- **The new icons.** The app's banner is 16:9 (the shape Android TV and Google TV show on their home screens),
  made for every screen density (160 to 640 pixels wide), with the round LUMEN emblem and *TV*; the launcher
  symbol is the emblem on the dark blue tile, also as an adaptive icon. The Samsung and LG icons follow.
- **The icons in other shapes** are in `branding/`: 16:9 (1920×1080, 1280×720, 640×360), 4:3, 2:1 and square (1024, 512),
  with and without the *TV*. `tools/make_icons.py` makes all of them again from the emblem.
- **A new README** with screenshots of the app and what the Android app can do on its own.
