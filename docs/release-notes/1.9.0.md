# Lumen TV 1.9.0

## What is new

- **A disc with its own menu.** On the page of a MakeMKV disc there is a new row, *Disc mit ihrem Menü abspielen*:
  the disc starts as it would in a player – the studio logos, then the menu with its background video and its sound.
  The remote works as on a disc player: arrows and OK move through the buttons, *Back* leaves the menu again (and gives the
  drive back). The menu is drawn by libbluray inside the app (decrypted through MakeMKV's library), the picture and
  sound go to the television's own player.
- **Fixed on the way:** the sound of a disc changes its layout between clips (6 channels in the studio logo, stereo
  in the next): the player then stood still. The FFmpeg decoder now keeps the layout it announced, and the clips are put
  one after the other without the hole in time that the disc's clocks left (the stock decoder also crashed on a null
  pointer here; its patch is in `tools/ffmpeg_jni.patch`).
- The player's loader no longer stops on the size of its buffer while the sound is still missing.

## Not in this version

- Menus written in Java (BD-J) – the disc is shown, but its buttons do not work.
- The menu takes about a minute to open (the disc is read and decrypted first), and it needs the memory of the whole
  television: other apps are closed meanwhile.
- Needs MakeMKV's files (as the rest of the MakeMKV features); without them nothing changes.
