# Lumen TV 1.7.0

## What is new

- **Changing the audio or subtitle track during a disc from MakeMKV, and skipping back a few seconds.**
  The stream is now indexed as it arrives (clusters of the Matroska file), so the player can go back to
  just before the picture it shows – as far as the cache reaches – instead of starting at byte 0, which
  a stream cannot do. Listen through the tracks (Up / Menu) to tell, for instance, an old dub from a
  new one; the choice is kept for the disc.
- **The track lists say what the disc says.** A DTS-HD MA track that MakeMKV lists twice (the track and
  the DTS core inside it) is shown once. Subtitles are numbered, with their *only forced parts* copy
  beside each. The disc's own stream table (language, format, channels, secondary audio) is read and
  logged to check what MakeMKV reports.
- **The disc's picture fills its tile** to the edge, the name on a dark foot.
- *Read the disc again* (under *More options*).

## Still not possible

- Telling an old dub from a new one, or a commentary from the film, by name: discs do not say, they show
  it in their own menu (BD-J). Listening is the way; the disc's table is shown as it is.
- Chapter pictures, 3D output (DLP-Link), BD-J menus.
