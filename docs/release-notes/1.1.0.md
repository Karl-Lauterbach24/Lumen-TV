# Lumen TV 1.1.0

First release: receiver apps for [Lumen](https://github.com/Karl-Lauterbach24/Lumen) 1.1.0 and later.

| Platform | File |
|----------|------|
| Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV | `LumenTV-1.1.0-android.apk` (debug-signed) |
| LG TV (webOS) | `io.github.lumen.tv_1.1.0_all.ipk` |
| Samsung TV (Tizen) | `LumenTV-1.1.0-tizen-unsigned.wgt` (has to be signed with your own Samsung certificate) |

How to install: [docs/install.md](https://github.com/Karl-Lauterbach24/Lumen-TV/blob/main/docs/install.md).
Any other device can open the address from Lumen's Cast dialog in its browser instead.

## Notes

- **None of the apps has been run on a real television.** The protocol, the stream and the receiver page are
  tested automatically in the Lumen repository, including playback in a desktop browser; the Android app is
  only compiled. Reports are welcome.
- The picture arrives three to four seconds after the computer shows it. It is 1080p or 720p SDR with stereo
  sound.
- Texts in 16 languages, machine-assisted except English and German.
