# Lumen TV 1.17.0

- **New player controls**, laid out for the remote: title and source at the top with the clock and when the film ends; a time bar with the disc's chapters as gaps, what is buffered and – with MakeMKV – what the cache holds; Left/Right look for a place in growing steps with a bubble (time, chapter) and jump when the key is let go; Down leads to the buttons (chapter back/on, 10 s back/on, play/pause, audio and subtitles, the next title). A large play sign while paused, a ring while the stream is awaited. The first Back hides the controls, the second ends the film.
- **Chapters** come from the disc's playlists (their entry marks), read before MakeMKV takes the drive; for the app's own playback per clip. The chapter keys of the remote jump between them.
- **Fix: a stopped MakeMKV could hang the drive.** The app closed its USB connection while MakeMKV was still finishing a drive command (seen when a quick start was let go on leaving a disc page; only switching the drive off helped). Now MakeMKV ends first and the connection is closed after it; the next start waits for that. The drive shim also holds SIGPIPE, SIGHUP and SIGINT until a command is answered, as it did SIGTERM.
- **A drive that has stopped answering** is now said so on its page ("switch it off and on") instead of an old "MakeMKV does not know this disc's key".

Tested on the TV (Changhong, Android 11): the controls with a file (seeking, pause, buttons, track list). Not yet tested on the TV: chapters from a real disc and the new stop sequence with MakeMKV (the drive was hung by the old stop at the time).
