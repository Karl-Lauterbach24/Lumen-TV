# Lumen TV 1.14.0

## What is new (a disc played with its own menu)

- **The sound the disc chose.** A transport stream says nothing about languages, so the player took the track with the most
  channels – English 5.1 where the disc plays German. bdmenu now passes on only the audio stream the disc selects (its PSR 1, looked
  up in the current play item's stream table); when the disc's menu changes the language, the stream changes with it.
- **No still picture between the logos and the menu.** Where the streams change (Dolby Digital in the logos, DTS-HD in the menu)
  the next stream is queued at once, and the player prepares it while it plays the end of the old one.
- **Logos without stutter.** Where a disc goes from clip to clip, each new clip now begins after the later end of picture *and*
  sound; set from the sound alone, its pictures fell before the last ones and the player dropped them as late (845 pictures in ten
  seconds in Doctor Who's logos, now 32 in a minute).

## Fixed

- **"Cannot create AudioTrack" (Dolby Digital):** after a restart this television reports Dolby Digital passthrough and then refuses
  the track; sound is now always decoded to PCM by the app.
- 1.13's gentle stop of MakeMKV listed `/proc` to find its programs and crashed the app: this television has an entry there whose name
  is not valid UTF-8, and Android aborts on it. The programs are found with `ps` now.
