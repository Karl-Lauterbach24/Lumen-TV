# Lumen TV 1.17.1

- **Simpler controls**: OK shows the controls and pauses, Up shows them without pausing, Down hides them; without a key they go after 5 seconds. Shown, Left/Right go through the buttons and Up leads to the time bar; hidden, Left/Right look for a place at once. Audio and subtitles sit on the far left, the next episode or title on the far right.
- **One MakeMKV run at a time**: a quick start and the reading of the disc's menu could start in the same moment and open the drive's USB connection twice, closing it under each other; the drive hung soon after (seen with Doctor Who season 2, disc 2). Now each run waits for the one before.
- **An unplugged drive ends its run**: MakeMKV and the menu player stop when the drive is gone (before, the menu player kept asking the vanished drive for 25 minutes), and the film ends with "The drive was disconnected".
- **Bonus material in standard definition** (576p/480p on a Blu-ray with HD titles) is listed with the extras, never as the film or an episode. A series disc with one episode-long title is shown as that episode, named by the episode guide.
- **More look-ahead for MakeMKV films**: the player keeps up to 40 MB (10–30 s) instead of 12 MB (about 2 s at the bit rate of 1080p with DTS-HD MA).
- Chapters also after a quick start; the "Starting …" message goes once the picture runs; "1 episode" instead of "1 episodes".

Tested on the TV (Changhong, Android 11) with Doctor Who season 2: the new keys and layout, chapters after a quick start, stopping MakeMKV (during a quick start, during playback, on leaving a disc page), the SD bonus title as an extra. Not yet tested: unplugging the drive during playback; whether the larger look-ahead removes the short hitches seen right after the start (measured: one audio underrun and 14 dropped frames in the first 22 s).
