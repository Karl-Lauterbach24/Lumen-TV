# Lumen TV 1.4.2

## What is new

- **Episodes of a disc reckoned like a real box.** Where a season does not divide evenly over its discs,
  the episodes left over are taken to lie on the first discs (13 episodes, 3 per disc: 4 + 3 + 3 + 3, so
  disc 3 holds episodes 8–10). Where a box is laid out otherwise, *Episodes … wrong? One on* and *One
  episode back* shift the numbers; the choice is kept for that disc.
- The disc page and the messages around MakeMKV and the key database are in German where the
  television is.
