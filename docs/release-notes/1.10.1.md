# Lumen TV 1.10.1

## What is new

- **Playing the disc with its menu is there at once** – on the disc page as the first row, before the disc has been
  identified (the menu needs no titles). If the titles are being read at that moment, that is stopped and the drive freed.
- **A disc whose key MakeMKV does not know** says so also when its menu is started (it used to end in "the stream could not
  be read").
