# Lumen TV 1.16.0

## What is new

- **Audio CDs.** A CD with audio tracks is recognised by its table of contents (READ TOC) and listed track by track with its
  length; each track plays as a WAV stream of the drive's raw audio (READ CD, 2352-byte sectors), seekable. The drive is set to 4×
  CD speed for it, which is quiet and four times what the music needs. Not yet tried with a CD in the drive tested.
- **HD DVDs go to MakeMKV** (EVO in HVDVD_TS, AACS), like protected Blu-rays. The ASUS drive tested cannot read HD DVD; with a
  drive that can (the Xbox 360 HD DVD drive on USB), MakeMKV reads them.
- **Episode names for Doctor Who's second season**: TVmaze's specials are asked for (`?specials=1`); disc 1 now reads
  "Special · The Christmas Invasion", "Episode 1 · New Earth".
- Long waits ("reading the disc", "starting", "opening the disc's menu") show a running bar.

## What does not play (and why)

- **Data CDs** (ISO 9660 without UDF: MP3 or video files on a CD-R) – the app reads UDF only, which every DVD and Blu-ray has.
- **Video CDs** – MPEG in Mode 2 sectors, which needs raw reads like an audio CD; not built.
- **4K pictures** on this television (its decoder stops at 1920×1088), see 1.13.1.
