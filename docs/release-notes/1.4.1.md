# Lumen TV 1.4.1

## What is new

- **Series discs are named episode by episode.** After *Identify the disc*, a series disc shows its
  show, season and disc (e.g. *Doctor Who (2005) · Season 1 · Disc 3*) and its episodes by number and
  title (*Episode 7 · The Long Game* …), from TVmaze (only the series' name is sent; the show whose
  episode lengths match the disc is taken; which episodes a disc holds is reckoned from its number).
  *All episodes in a row* and the extras follow. A film disc shows *Play the film*.
- **Audio and subtitles are chosen before playing** on the disc page (automatic, off, or a language of
  the disc); while playing, *Up* or *Menu* still opens the track list.
- **Even playback from LibreDrive discs (MakeMKV, experimental).** MakeMKV now reads the drive only as
  fast as the film is played: before, it kept what it read ahead in memory without a limit, and a
  television with 1 GB ran out of memory within a minute. Measured on such a television: an episode
  played minutes long without a single stall or dropped frame, MakeMKV staying at about 100 MB.
- The disc page and its entries are in German where the television is.

## Fixes

- Stopping a film played through MakeMKV no longer closes the app.
- A disc page shows the result of an action (identifying, choosing a language) at once.
