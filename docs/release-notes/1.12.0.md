# Lumen TV 1.12.0

## What is new

- **A disc's menu goes on when the sound changes its format.** Menus and films often use different sound (Dolby Digital in the logos,
  DTS-HD in the menu and the episodes) on the same stream number. The player cannot change a track's format in the middle of a stream;
  it failed, the menu ended (and a key pressed then went to the app's page, which opened the menu again – likely what made
  "start the film" in Evangelion's menu not start it). bdmenu now ends the stream where the streams change and goes on in a new fifo;
  the app plays the old stream out and opens the new one.
- **DTS-HD Master Audio in the disc menu path is played as its DTS core.** The player's DTS reader mistook parts of the extension for
  DTS Express and its decoder failed; bdmenu removes the extension (also where a disc carries it in PES of its own). DTS Express
  itself is now decoded by FFmpeg too.
- The drive bridge between the app and MakeMKV is one per process: when the activity was made anew (the drive plugged in again),
  MakeMKV got no drive ("Address already in use").
- bdmenu says why it ended (end of the disc's data, the player gone, told to quit).

## Not yet tested

- Choosing an episode or "play all" in a disc's menu and watching it to the end; Evangelion's "start film" with this version.
