# Lumen TV 1.15.0

## What is new

- **Quick start.** While a disc's page is open, its main title (the film, the first episode) is prepared in the background:
  MakeMKV's scan of the disc (about half a minute) and the first seconds of the cache. Started then, it plays at once – measured
  5.8 s from the key to the picture instead of 44 s. Choosing something else ends the preparation. *Settings › Disc and MakeMKV ›
  Quick start* switches it off.
- **A cache of minutes.** The cache for MakeMKV's stream is now 384 MB in storage where there is room (it was 32 MB in memory on a
  television with 1 GB): measured 130 MB – about a minute of film – ahead after two minutes, MakeMKV never held.
- **A quiet drive.** The drive reads at full speed while the cache fills (the start, after a jump) and at 2× Blu-ray speed
  (9000 KB/s, enough for any Blu-ray) once a third of the cache is ahead; the drive shim sets the speed the app asks for
  (`/tmp/.mmshim.speed`). MakeMKV delivers about 4 MB/s in the app at most, so full speed brings little but noise.
- **The film first.** On a disc's page the film or the episodes come first, the disc's own menu after them.
- **Episodes with a special before them**: a long special shown after a season's finale opens the next season, as on the boxes
  (Doctor Who's Christmas specials); short extras are left out.
