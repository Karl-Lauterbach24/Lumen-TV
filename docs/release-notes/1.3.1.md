# Lumen TV 1.3.1

## What is new

- **A *Disc* card on the home page, first in the row** (like the other Lumen apps), as soon as a disc drive
  is connected: OK opens the disc straight away, with *Play the disc with its menu* on top. With several
  discs it lists them.
- **The sounds of a disc's menu.** Blu-ray menus bring their own button sounds (`sound.bdmv`): they
  play when a button is selected or activated, as on a player. (Lumen's own interface sounds were already
  there for the pages of the app.)
- **A disc put into, or taken out of, a drive that is already connected is noticed** (the home page asks
  now and then); a drive that had been empty no longer stays "empty".
- Fix in the menu engine bridge: events the library hands out together with the stream data (sounds among
  them) were dropped.

## Checked, and limits

Run in the Android TV emulator with discs made for the purpose: the Disc card and its order, the menu
test disc with its button sounds (decoded and played, 80 ms and 160 ms tones as authored), key
navigation and activation. **Not tried**: a pressed retail disc, a real drive's disc swap, a real
television. Limits as in 1.3.0: no BD-J menus, no BD+, no seeking in a film started through its menu,
Ultra HD discs need a drive that can read them.
